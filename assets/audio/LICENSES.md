## `hearth_fire.ogg`

- Source: generated locally for this project; no third-party media was used.
- Generator: FFmpeg 8.1.1.
- License: original project asset with no external attribution requirement.
- Generation: combined filtered brown and pink noise with deterministic sparse crackle impulses, then crossfaded the tail into the head to form an 8.5-second mono 48 kHz loop and encoded it as Ogg Vorbis.
